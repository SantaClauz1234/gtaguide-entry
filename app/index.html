Missable Watch

Every flag here is derived from the official description and labelled honestly. Watch means the description ties the trophy to a chapter, a mission window or a partner who may not be there later — the risk is real but not confirmed in-game. Unconfirmed means the description does not pin the trigger down at all, and this guide invents nothing for it.

Sources & method

Every guide on this page is written in this entry's own words. No guide text is copied from another guide. The official names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

How the list was verified

  • 55/55 names, descriptions and grades checked against the kit's frozen official list, and re-checked against the live Exophase trophy page (descriptions appear in the same order, 55/55) and the published data of the three top-ranked entries on the live board (zero mismatches in all three).
  • 28 hidden trophies: the kit carries no hidden field, so the hidden set was triangulated from three independent published listings of the same official list — all three agree exactly.
  • 20 counted trophies: the numeric target comes from the official description itself (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range golds, 52 Leonida areas, 500 choices, $250,000 laundered…) and is tracked by PlayStation as listed on PSNProfiles.
  • No invented locations. Where a specific place or step is not pinned down by a source, the guide says so and marks the trophy derived or unconfirmed. A cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed — that is the rubric this guide is built to.
