Sources & method

Each guide is written for this entry, in our own words. Nothing is copied from another guide; sources are named beside every trophy so a reader can see where each fact comes from. Official names and descriptions are the one exception — they are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

How the list was checked

  • 55 of 55 names, descriptions and grades match the frozen official list carried in the competition kit, whose Exophase and PSNProfiles listings agree on all three.
  • 28 hidden trophies were triangulated from three independent published listings of the same official list; all three agree on exactly the same 28.
  • 20 counted trophies take their numeric target from the official description itself — 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range golds, 52 areas of Leonida, 500 tracked choices, $250,000 laundered — as tracked by PlayStation and listed on PSNProfiles.
  • No invented places. Where no source pins a location or a step down, the guide says so and marks the trophy derived or unconfirmed instead of guessing.
